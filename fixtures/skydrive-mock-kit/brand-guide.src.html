SkyDrive brand guide

Version 1.0 (mock) · October 2026

Logo

Main logo: use on white and light backgrounds.
Logo for dark backgrounds: hangar screens, dark headers.
Icon: app icons, favicons and small spaces only.
  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the canopy.
  • Never stretch, recolour, outline or add effects to the logo.
  • The smallest the full logo may appear is 120 px wide on screen, 1 in. in print.
  • On photos, use the dark-background version on a dark area only.

Colour

Sky blue
#5eb0ff · primary

Canopy orange
#ffb454 · accent

Night
#0b1220 · text, dark grounds

Cloud
#e8eefc · text on dark

Sky blue is the brand colour: buttons, links and headings. Canopy orange is an accent for small highlights only, never for warnings.

Typography

Headings: Inter Bold. Body: Inter Regular, or the system font where Inter is not available. Emails use the system font.

Voice

Plain, calm and specific. Say what is happening and what to do next. No jargon a first-time tandem customer would not know.
